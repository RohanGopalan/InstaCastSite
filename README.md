## Overview
This website is a companion to my team's 2025 HOSA Medical Innovation project, called InstaCast. The purpose of the cast was to allow for easy stabilization of injuries by simply wrapping it around your wrist, injecting the 2 part solution to conform it around the injury, and tapping your phone to the built-in NFC tag to open this site and provide key medical information to the nearest hospital with just your voice. We worked with Saint Louis University's (SLU) Biomedical Engineering department, specifically under the guidance of Dr. Gary Bledsoe and his research assistants to develop the 2 part solution such that it reacted quickly in an emergency and was fluid enough to run all throughout the cast. Additionally, to fabricate a medical-grade cast that was durable and flexible, we partnered with SLU-CAM (Center for Additive Manufacturing). Under their expertise, we learned about resin printers and industry-used CAD software and tools. In the end, we were able to create a cast made of a flexible photopolymer urethane resin in the form of a lattice structure which had "veins" on the inside to evenly distribute the 2 part solution. We presented InstaCast at the Missouri HOSA State Leadership Conference and won 1st place in Medical Innovation. This qualified us for the International Leadership Conference in Nashville, TN during the summer. We further improved the 2 part solution, lattice structure, and our presentation and were eliminated as finalists.
